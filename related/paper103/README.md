# Beyond Pixel Overlap

**A Structural and Topological Audit of Dice Limitations in Retinal Vessel Segmentation**

Rafael Albuquerque, Hitalo Silva, Arlington Rodrigues, Diego Pinheiro and
Carmelo Bastos-Filho
PPGEC, Universidade de Pernambuco (UPE), Recife, Brazil

IEEE Latin American Conference on Computational Intelligence, LA-CCI 2026,
Lima, Peru. **Paper 103.**

Correspondence: rafael.balbuquerque@upe.br

---

**This is not our work.** It is hosted here with the authors' permission
because it was presented at the same conference. Paper 69, the subject of
the rest of this repository, is a separate study with different authors and
a different topic.

The deck argues that Dice and IoU are structurally blind: four losses reach
near-identical Dice (0.787 to 0.808) while fragmenting the vessel tree into
anywhere from 43.7 to 97.4 connected components. It proposes a
multidimensional audit, including a Thin-to-Thick Vessel Ratio, Betti
errors and network-science descriptors on the vessel graph.
